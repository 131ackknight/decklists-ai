## How the data is kept (2026-11-07, after the owner: "We arent very careful with our data are we?")

- **PROGRESS.md is generated, never edited:** `pnpm exec tsx scripts/scoreboard.ts` after every run or judging round. Its only hand-kept part is `evals/progress-log-only.md` (numbers with no result file).
- **Every paid run records itself** in `evals/runs.jsonl` (started and finished, with commit, cost and result file). A started line with no finished line is a crashed run. Past runs were backfilled from their files (`scripts/backfill-runs.ts`).
- **Results save after every item.** A crashed `run-round4.ts` run is finished with `--resume`; it refuses to overwrite an existing results file.
- **Tests are frozen** in `evals/tests.ts` (items, parameters, data cutoff 2026-10-20, fingerprint). `pnpm test` fails if one changes; a change means a new version (tuning-v2), not an edit.
- **Export every judging round right after the owner finishes:** ArtifactData `list` on the page URL and its collection (`decks`, `pairs` or `ratings`) with `out_dir` `evals/answers/<NN-slug>`, then add the page to `evals/answers/pages.json`. `pnpm test` fails if a key file has no export. Scripts read verdicts through `evals/answers.ts`, never hand-copied.

## Rules the owner set (keep to them)

- **Generic tools that give specific knowledge. Never fixes bespoke to one card or engine.**
- **The AI never learns from the owner's notes or verdicts.** It would make the owner's judging circular. The notes in `evals/owner-notes/` are a record only.
- Use the same prompts when checking whether something improved; use fresh prompts to check it generalizes.
- Two runs per version on the future test before believing a difference.
- Plain, simple language with the owner. Short answers. Show data in small tables.
- Keep the Mac awake for runs (`request_keep_awake`); a closed lid kills runs.

## What we learned (one line each)

- Knowledge about these specific cards or this plan helps (v5 format menu, v8 how real decks run the cards, v10 win line, v11 dominant shell, v12 enabler counts).
- Generic additions never helped: extra stages (v3), a coach (v4), copy limits (v6), example brews (v7), a mana-base tool (v14), format staples (v15), a synergy shortlist (v16).
- Lands are flat at 47% match in every version; wrong colors on about half the decks.
- Most of the match gain since v2 was copying a better old deck; what the AI adds beyond its closest old list is flat at about +11 (inventors +29).
- That gap is mostly spells borrowed from other decks: inventors add 10 copies and 53% stay; the AI 4, and 21% stay. Mostly little-played spells (under 2% of lists), not staples. PROGRESS.md section 2g.
- A list of the little-played spells real decks play alongside the requested cards (in the deck's colors) holds a third of the spells inventors borrowed at 20 cards and half at 50 (`scripts/candidate-check.ts`); synergy and per-job lists do no better than most-played-first. Opus decks already hold 15%.
- The biggest spell gap is the "hidden gem": the under-played card the human brewer found (16-17 of 51 missing points). The AI skips them even when shown.
- No kind of card is the weak spot (2026-11-10, `scripts/point-breakdown.ts`, free): v22 matches 40-47% of lands, creatures, removal and card draw alike; inventors 60-70% of each. 54 of the 58 missing points are cards not played at all, not copy counts. Named cards 97%; other spells 25% (inventors 56%). A vote over 3 builds adds nothing (`scripts/consensus-check.ts`): builds share 46 of 60 cards, so the misses are the same every run. Lands from data fail even with the real deck's colors (9.6 shared vs the AI's own 10.2).
- Colors (same script): v22 adds a color the named cards don't have in 35% of builds and is then right 18% of the time (82% when it stays in the named cards' colors); the named cards' colors alone are right on 71% of decks, v22 on 56%, v0 on 55%. A color rule (stay in the named cards' colors) was then checked on the saved builds (`scripts/color-rule-check.ts`): it fixes 3 decks and breaks Aang, where the AI was right to add blue; right colors 56% to 60-67%, about +0.2 to +0.5 match. Not built.
- Lands (2026-11-10, `scripts/land-fix-check.ts`, free): the brewer plays too many basics (about 12 in two-color Standard decks; real 3-8) and skips color-fixing lands. `src/harness/lands.ts` learns lands by cycle and role from every recent deck with the same number of colors. Best of six code-only fixes (swap basics above the usual count for the usual fixers the deck lacks): lands shared +0.38 (+0.05 to +0.71), match +0.59 (-0.00 to +1.17), Added +0.57 (-0.01 to +1.15); not kept (misses the rule by 0.01, best of six tries). Replacing all lands is worse than the brewer's own. Land note test (2026-11-10, `scripts/land-note-test.ts`, $0.41): with the AI making the swaps from a note, lands shared +0.45 (-0.05 to +0.96) on 21 builds against the code-only swap's +0.56, so the code-only swap (`landAdvice` + `applyLandSwaps`) is the version to carry forward. The AI rightly refused on one-color decks, so `landAdvice` now skips them: all 63 builds lands +0.37 (+0.04 to +0.71), match +0.57 (-0.02 to +1.16), Added +0.56 (-0.02 to +1.13). Not kept by protocol-v4 (a hair short on Added). Built into the brewer as v23 (2026-11-10) and checked on decks it wasn't picked on: it loses lands there (-0.74 on 6 future-test decks; Premodern Rector Combo loses 3 to 5 lands when its basics are swapped for Gemstone Mine and pain lands). The dev gain is two-color Standard decks (+1.3 lands per changed build); with three or more colors, or in older formats, real decks keep basics the vote can't explain. Narrowing the swap after seeing this would be fitting to the test decks; not done.
- **A rule picked on the dev decks gets a free check on the future test's saved brews before any recommendation** (`scripts/land-swap-check.ts` is the pattern: the code step on saved builds, all decks and the ones that are not dev archetypes; 8 of future-v2's 14 decks are dev archetypes, 6 are not).
- Nonbasic lands (2026-11-10, free: `scripts/nonbasic-land-check.ts`, `land-lookup-check.ts`, `land-cut-check.ts`, `beside-colors-check.ts`): lands follow the deck. The brewer plays about as many nonbasic lands as real decks (13.2 vs 16.9) but half are other lands: 6.5 shared (38%; inventors 66%). The biggest gap to inventors is the deck's own lands that nobody in the format was playing (3.0 missed vs 1.2), in decks where the brewer built a different deck. Any list of lands it could be shown (beside the requested cards, the list it built on, usual for its colors, format staples) holds 19-63% of the missed copies but is 9-49% right, so showing lists is v20's problem again. Putting back lands cut from the list it built on is right 44% of the time on dev and 23% on unseen decks. Colors from the lists that run the requested cards are right 24% / 21% (the brewer 65% / 46%, the requested cards' own mana symbols 67% / 64%). Owner, 2026-11-10: **"Yes, stop on lands"**. Don't propose another land lookup, rule or note without new evidence.
- The other gaps (2026-11-10, free; BUILD_LOG entry "Where are the other gaps?"): the 23 points to the inventors on dev are spells borrowed from other decks 7.3, lands added 5.1, the starting deck 4.5, wrong cuts 2.8, new cards 1.7, the rest 1.4. Checked and closed: the starting deck (`scripts/starting-list-check.ts`: data picks score 26.9 and 21.7 against the brewer's own 32.3; hindsight 42.7), sideboards (`scripts/sideboard-check.ts`: the brewer 21.6% of 15, inventors 32.3%, built from data 20.6%), mana in play (`scripts/mana-play-check.ts`: the brewer's mana works as well as the real decks' in the simulator). It is one gap: choosing which cards fit. Only playing the deck (v19) has pointed the other way, on the owner's blind panel (13 of 17 against 10), and the automatic score disagreed.
- Inventor rows count each deck once (fixed in `scripts/scoreboard.ts` 2026-11-10; they used to count every first-week list). Check any new script that averages over first-week lists does the same.
- Against the model alone (v0), the harness's edge is mostly knowing what's being played: match +9 to +24, most on decks after the training cutoff. What a deck adds beyond its closest old list gains only 4-7 points, and v0's own mistakes (made-up names, banned cards) cost about 3 at most.
